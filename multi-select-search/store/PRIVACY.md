# Privacy Policy — Multi-Select Tag Search

Last updated: 19 August 2026

## The short version

Multi-Select Tag Search does not operate a backend, does not use analytics, and does not store or transmit your browsing activity or selections to us.

When you activate the extension, it temporarily processes information from the current page in your browser only to provide the extension's multi-select search functionality.

No account is required.

## What the extension processes

When you click the extension's toolbar icon on a supported page, the extension temporarily reads the visible tag labels, category labels, and relevant links on that page.

This information is used only to:

- identify the selectable tags or categories;
- allow you to select multiple items; and
- build the combined search you request.

This processing takes place locally in your browser.

The extension does not write this information to chrome.storage, localStorage, IndexedDB, cookies, or any external database.

Your selections are temporary and are cleared when the extension is deactivated, the page is reloaded, or the tab is closed.

## Browsing activity

The extension does not monitor, record, or retain your browsing history.

It uses Chrome's activeTab permission, which provides temporary access to the current tab only after you explicitly activate the extension.

When activated, the extension may temporarily access the current page URL and page content as necessary to:

- determine whether the current website or page is supported;
- identify selectable tags or categories; and
- construct the search requested by you.

This information is not transmitted to us or stored by the extension.

The extension also uses Chrome's declarativeContent functionality to adjust its availability or appearance on supported websites without continuously reading your browsing activity.

## Network activity

The extension does not send page content, browsing activity, selections, or usage information to us, to an analytics provider, or to any external service operated on our behalf.

When you press "Find matches," the extension creates and opens a search URL on the website you are already using.

That search URL may contain or represent the tags or categories you selected. Your browser sends that request directly to the relevant website as part of the search you explicitly requested.

The handling of that request by the website is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect or store:

- personally identifiable information;
- browsing history;
- search history;
- location information;
- authentication information;
- personal communications;
- financial information;
- health information;
- usage analytics; or
- advertising identifiers.

The current version of the extension has no analytics system or developer-operated backend.

## Feedback and site requests

"Send feedback" and "Suggest a site" open a pre-filled draft in your own email application.

Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email yourself. You can edit or delete the message before sending it.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you voluntarily include in that message, together with the email address from which you send it.

We use that information only to respond to you, address feedback, and evaluate requests for additional supported websites.

## Children

Multi-Select Tag Search is a general-purpose browsing utility.

The extension does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those changes, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

Any material change to how user data is handled will also be reflected in the extension's applicable Chrome Web Store disclosures.
